A normal chess game — filmed.

1 · Play — normal chess: click a piece, then a highlighted square. Hover a target to read the live pros & cons. Every move leaves a glowing path, so you always see the whole move.
2 · Shoot — you have 60 seconds of video in total. Hit REC (or Space) and tune 12 parameters: lens, aperture, frame rate, shutter angle, ISO, ND, focus, white balance, saturation, tilt, resolution, orbit, stabilizer.
3 · Score — every 0.1 s frame is scored out of 100 (exposure, shutter, focus, composition, action…). Your video score is the average over the full 60 s, so use all of it. Best reel wins — not the best chess. Replay any reel in Terminator-vision with the full technical read-out.
